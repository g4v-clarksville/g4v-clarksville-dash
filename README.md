# Guitars for Vets — Clarksville Chapter #287

> Official digital portal, student progress tracking, and searchable song/chord library for G4V Chapter #287 in Clarksville, TN.

Welcome to the official repository and web application codebase for **Guitars for Vets (G4V) Chapter #287** located in Clarksville, Tennessee. This project houses our chapter's digital web interface, interactive song and chord libraries, and administrative tracking tools designed to support our local veteran community.

---

## 🎸 Mission Architecture
Guitars for Vets is a national 501(c)(3) non-profit organization founded in 2007. Chapter #287, established in **February 2026**, is dedicated to providing a therapeutic musical outlet for combat veterans and service members struggling with PTSD, physical injuries, and emotional distress. 

Through structured, one-on-one guitar instruction and community jam sessions, we aim to restore feelings of joy, purpose, and lifelong peer support.

## 📘 The Three-Pillar Program Model
1. **Free Lessons:** 10 weeks of private, individualized guitar instruction tailored strictly to each veteran's pace and skill level.
2. **Free Gear:** Complete graduation bundle provided upon program completion (brand-new acoustic guitar, electronic tuner, capo, strap, picks, and gig bag).
3. **Community Jams:** Integration into ongoing group sessions and live amplifier jam circles to sustain long-term musical camaraderie.

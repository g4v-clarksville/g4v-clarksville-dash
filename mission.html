Who We Are

Guitars for Vets (G4V) is a national 501(c)3 non-profit organization which operates more than 150 chapters in over 40 states. Since 2007, we have refined a guitar lesson program that provides Veterans living with the challenges of PTSD and other injuries a unique alternative to healing. Our program features 1,000 volunteers that deliver the mission at Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) facilities, community-based medical centers, Vet Centers, and Veterans Service Organizations.

Our Impact

What We Do

G4V puts the healing power of music in the hands of heroes by restoring feelings of joy and purpose that Veterans can lose after suffering traumatic experiences. The teamwork and camaraderie assists Vets as they reintegrate into their communities.

01

Acoustic Therapy

Providing structured, one-on-one and group guitar instruction designed to refocus the mind, ease anxiety, and build new cognitive pathways.

02

Lifelong Instruments

Upon graduation of our core program, veterans receive a brand new acoustic guitar and accessories package to sustain their musical journey.

03

Camaraderie & Jam

Creating safe, supportive peer environments via twice-monthly jam sessions at VFW Post #4895 in Clarksville, Tennessee.
